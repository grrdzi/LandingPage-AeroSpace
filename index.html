Mission Timeline

Our roadmap to establishing lunar presence

Orbital Test Flight

First orbital test of LUNARX-1 rocket system with full recovery and reuse demonstration.

Lunar Orbit Mission

Uncrewed circumlunar mission to test navigation, communication, and life support systems.

First Lunar Landing

Deployment of initial habitat module and power systems at the Shackleton Crater site.

Crewed Mission

First crew of 4 astronauts to inhabit the lunar base for a 30-day mission.

Base Expansion

Deployment of additional habitat modules, research facilities, and resource processing units.

Permanent Settlement

Establishment of first permanent lunar colony with rotating crews of 20 personnel.
